AI Interview Rubric

Evaluating Candidates in AI-Assisted Technical Interviews

Agenda

  • Overview & Assessment Scale
  • AI Experience
  • Requirements & Design
  • Implementation
  • Testing
  • How to Use This Rubric

Rubric Structure

  • Part 1: AI Experience — Early discussion, open-ended exploration of candidate background
  • Part 2: Solution Execution — Observe application across Requirements & Design, Implementation, Testing phases

Assessment Scale

  • Low: Struggling, missing fundamentals, red flags
  • Medium: Capable, demonstrates competence, room to grow
  • High: Strong, demonstrates mastery, clear growth trajectory
AI Experience: Landscape

Knowledge of tools, models, and current capabilities

🔴 Low
Only knows ChatGPT or chat mode; can't explain model differences; no awareness of tool strengths/weaknesses
🟡 Medium
Knows 2-3 tools/models; can explain basic differences; has opinions on when to use what
🟢 High
Deep knowledge of multiple tools and models; understands tradeoffs; aware of emerging patterns
AI Experience: Journey

Understanding of AI evolution and capability inflection points

🔴 Low
Thinks AI capability is static; no awareness of recent changes; hasn't thought about trajectory
🟡 Medium
Aware capability has improved over time; can point to recent shifts; understands impact on strategy
🟢 High
Deep understanding of AI timeline and inflection points; conscious of Dec 2025+ jump; actively adapting
AI Experience: Agency

Has the candidate actually built and shared?

🔴 Low
Only reactive chat usage; no projects built; hasn't experimented or shared
🟡 Medium
Built a few AI projects (personal or work); experimented outside work; may have helped a coworker
🟢 High
Multiple projects built; actively experimenting; teaching/helping others; building AI-based tools
AI Experience: Controls

Knowledge of AI primitives: skills, rules, agents, orchestrators

🔴 Low
Only knows basic prompting; can't explain or use skills, rules, agents; single interaction model
🟡 Medium
Aware of skills, rules, agents as concepts; has used one or more in practice; understands when to apply
🟢 High
Proficient with multiple primitives; has built custom skills/rules/agents; orchestrates strategically
Requirements & Design

Observable Signals

🔴 Low
Accepts AI output without pushback; doesn't ask follow-up questions; seems unsure of what they want
🟡 Medium
Asks AI to adjust or refine; makes some requests for changes; iterates a bit
🟢 High
Clearly states what they want/don't want; pushes back on suggestions; directs iteratively
Implementation

Observable Signals

🔴 Low
Blindly accepts code; doesn't read output; says "looks good" without inspection
🟡 Medium
Reads the code; spots some issues; makes minor requests; makes at least one change
🟢 High
Carefully reviews structure; identifies missing pieces; makes deliberate changes showing understanding
Testing

Observable Signals

🔴 Low
Doesn't review tests; accepts blindly; no additions or changes
🟡 Medium
Reviews tests; makes requests; adds some edge cases; contributes to suite
🟢 High
Critically evaluates test inputs; identifies gaps; adds meaningful edge cases; shows domain knowledge

How to Use This Rubric

  • Part 1 (AI Experience): Assess during intro/discussion — listen for claims about tools, projects, and controls
  • Part 2 (Solution): Observe during problem-solving — watch how they direct, iterate, and think critically
  • Not everything gets demonstrated: Some knowledge won't show up in the time available — that's normal
  • Quick assessment: Use the scale (Low/Medium/High) for rapid scoring during or after the interview

Key insight: Rubric evaluates how well candidates demonstrate the Core Principles from the candidate guide in action.
