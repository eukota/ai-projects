# Background-job dashboard

A user submits a simulated report job, watches its status change, and retrieves the result.

## Minimum scope

Submit a simulated report job, show its status, and retrieve the result.

## Follow-up change

Add failure and retry behavior while preserving job history. This exposes any one-run-per-job assumption made during the build.

## Timing

Solve once, then change. See [medium pacing](../../01-shared/02-medium-pacing.md): 10 clarify and plan, 30 build, 15 test and revise, 5 tradeoffs.
