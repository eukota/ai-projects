# Student prompt

You are building a small tool for a class office-hours session. Students join a help queue, and the instructor works through it.

## Request

Build a web app where:

- A student enters their name and a short question, and joins the queue.
- The student sees where they are in the queue.
- The instructor can mark the person at the front as helped.

The app has a browser UI and an HTTP API with in-memory storage. A starter app is provided.

## What we want to see

Before you build, say what you would ask the interviewer. Then direct the AI, read what it produces, and show how you know it works.

You have about 25 minutes. Work as you would in an AI-assisted interview: state assumptions, explain your choices, and verify behavior.
