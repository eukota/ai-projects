# Office-hours queue

Students join a help queue and see their position. The instructor marks people as helped.

## Minimum scope

Join a help queue, see your position, and let the instructor mark someone helped.

## Interview judgment

Define ordering and prevent accidental duplicate submissions. Both are underspecified in the one-line request: what counts as “the same student”, and what happens to positions when someone is helped.

## Timing

Two attempts of about 25 minutes plus a 10-minute comparison. See [small pacing](../../01-shared/01-small-pacing.md).
