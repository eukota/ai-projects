# Question bank

Full-stack exercises for the 60-minute build segment of the two-hour AI interview class. Each exercise practices interview behavior with an AI: clarify requirements, direct the AI, explain decisions, review generated code, and verify results.

## Contents

- [01-shared](./01-shared/) — pacing, comparison rubric, and starter-app assumptions used by every exercise.
- [02-small](./02-small/) — 8 problems solved twice in the hour with different approaches.
- [03-medium](./03-medium/) — 8 problems solved once, then changed to expose an assumption.

## Choosing an exercise

| Situation | Pick |
| --- | --- |
| New students, or a topic that needs a clear contrast | Small: run two approaches and compare |
| Experienced students, or a topic about iteration and diagnosis | Medium: one build, then the follow-up change |
| No preference | Small 04 (office-hours queue) or medium 01 (appointment booking), the suggested starters |

## Status

Fully written: small 04 and medium 01. All others are stubs holding the scope and judgment point; each needs a student prompt, acceptance criteria, instructor checkpoints, and extensions (small) or a follow-up change (medium).

## Exercise file layout

Every fully written exercise folder contains:

1. `README.md` — scope, interview judgment, and timing notes.
2. `01-student-prompt.md` — what students receive.
3. `02-acceptance-criteria.md` — observable pass/fail behavior.
4. `03-instructor-checkpoints.md` — what to watch for and when to intervene.
5. `04-extensions.md` (small) or `04-follow-up-change.md` (medium).
