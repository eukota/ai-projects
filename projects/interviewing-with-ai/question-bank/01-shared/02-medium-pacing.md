# Medium exercise pacing

Medium problems are solved once, then changed to show diagnosis, revision, and verification.

| Block | Minutes | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clarify and plan | 10 | Questions, assumptions, record shape, endpoints |
| Build | 30 | One complete user flow, end to end |
| Test and revise | 15 | Introduce the follow-up change, diagnose, fix, verify |
| Tradeoffs | 5 | What was assumed, what was risky, what to say in an interview |

## Order matters

Finish one end-to-end flow before introducing the follow-up change. The change should expose an assumption made during the build; introducing it early removes that effect.

## Follow-up changes are stretch goals

If the build overruns, state the follow-up change aloud and walk through how you would diagnose and verify it instead of implementing it. The reasoning is the interview skill.
